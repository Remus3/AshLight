## 4. Why not just move the target

The growth is concentrated, not spread. Five tests (one fixed window, one
fixed loop count, one sleep-bound family, two live calc presses) account
for most of the +21 s. Each one has a cheaper form that keeps its assert.
Moving the target to 140 s would accept growth that a few packages can
take back. It would also loosen the TEST-SLOW-BUDGET ratchet path (SUITE_S
260 -> 215 -> 200 s), which assumes the hold shrinks.

## 6. Ruling (adjudicator, 2026-11-22)

Call: BOTH. The reverse condition of the HOLD-75 amendment is treated as
met. Decision 0060 gets an interim gated-median bound of 145 s (soak bound
150 s) while 120 s stays the standing target. The five packages of
section 5 are filed to recover it. The bound goes back to 120 s only after
a formal TEST-SUITE-SOAK median is at or under 125 s.

Alternatives rejected:
- Keep 120 s and file only the packages, with no interim bound. That
  leaves the gate in breach of its own bound with no number to judge
  against while the packages land.
- Move the target to about 140 s for good. That ratchets the budget to
  match the regression when 19 to 35 s of savings are already estimated.
- Wait for a formal soak before ruling. Fourteen consecutive gated holds
  on the same instrument (the gate rows) are better evidence than a
  3-run soak.

Why: the suite grew 10.5 percent but the hold grew about 18.5 percent
(117.3 to 139.0 s). The cost sits in fixed timing windows and in the
one-worker calc group, not in the test count, so it can be fixed. The 145 s
bound sits above the 139 s median, and the 155.7 s outlier is a single run.
Order of work: (a) TEST-FUZZ-PARALLEL, (b) TEST-LOOP-WINDOW-5S and
(c) TEST-LUAJIT-RELOADS-12 first; then (d) TEST-CALC-GROUP-SPLIT, the
riskiest because of the shared warm bridge; (e) TEST-PROBE-XDIST-2 last.
Re-run the soak after (a) to (c) and again after (d). Do not promise
90 s.

Reverses if: a soak after the packages has a median at or under 125 s
(restore 120 s as the bound); (d) demonstrates under 90 s (tighten); or a
soak median exceeds 145 s (treat it as a regression and bisect, rather
than move the number again).
