TC-DRIFT and AREA-MATH (TC-BRIDGE / TC-DELTA). Pin under review:
config/tc_pins.json, PoB-PoE2 tag v0.23.1, commit 7d6f530c.

## Verdict

HOLD the pin. There is NO PoB-PoE2 release newer than v0.23.1. The pin
stays; no commit to config/tc_pins.json. The `dev` branch is 109 commits
ahead of v0.23.1 and carries game-data and trader work for patch 0.5.5
that no tagged release has yet, so the pinned calc lags the live game
(0.5.5f) for 0.5.5 rune / unique content. Re-check at the next tag; a large
release is likely before or at PoE2 1.0 on 2026-12-11 (see 0018).
